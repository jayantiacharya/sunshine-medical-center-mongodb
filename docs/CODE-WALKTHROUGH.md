# Code Walkthrough & Demo Requirements 🎥

This video is worth **30 points** and is a critical component of your submission. It demonstrates your understanding and ability to explain your implementation.

## What to Include in Your Video (6-8 minutes)

### 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
- Introduce yourself
- Brief overview of the project
- Technology stack used (Docker MongoDB, Node.js with MongoDB Driver)

### 2. Schema Design Explanation (2-3 minutes) 🏗️
- Show your collections in MongoDB Compass or mongosh
- Explain your key schema decisions:
  - Why you embedded certain data (prescriptions in treatments)
  - Why you used references (patient/doctor IDs in appointments)
  - Trade-offs you considered
- Show 1-2 sample documents from each collection

### 3. Code Walkthrough (2-3 minutes) 💻
- Walk through your seed script
- Show implementation of 2-3 interesting queries:
  - One complex find query with multiple conditions
  - One aggregation pipeline with multiple stages
  - One query using $lookup
- Explain your code organization and structure

### 4. Live Demo (2-3 minutes) 🚀
- Run your seed script (or show pre-populated database)
- Execute 3-5 queries live:
  - Basic CRUD operation
  - Complex query with filtering
  - Aggregation pipeline for analytics
- Show the results in your terminal or MongoDB Compass

### 5. Wrap-up (30 seconds)
- Summary of what you learned
- Challenges you overcame
- One thing you're proud of in your implementation

## Recording Requirements ⚙️

**Recommended Software** (Free):
- 🎬 [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) - Cross-platform, professional quality
- 🖥️ **macOS**: QuickTime Player (built-in)
- 🪟 **Windows**: Xbox Game Bar (built-in) or OBS Studio
- 🐧 **Linux**: SimpleScreenRecorder or OBS Studio

**Important Guidelines:**
- ⚠️ **Do NOT use your phone** to record the screen
- ✅ Use screen recording software with clear audio
- 🎤 Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- 📺 Ensure your screen is readable (appropriate zoom/font size)
- ⏱️ Keep it within 6-8 minutes (slightly under/over is okay)
- 📝 Consider writing a brief script or outline beforehand

## Evaluation Criteria 📊

Your video will be evaluated on:

### Code Explanation (15 points)
- Clear explanation of schema design decisions
- Good understanding of MongoDB concepts (embedding vs referencing)
- Explanation of query logic and aggregation pipelines
- Demonstration of code organization

### Demo Quality (10 points)
- All phases demonstrated (CRUD, Schema, Queries)
- Smooth execution without errors
- Clear demonstration of results
- Professional presentation

### Presentation (5 points)
- Clear audio and video quality
- Good pacing and organization
- Within time limit (6-8 minutes)
- Professional demeanor

## Tips for Success 💡

1. **Practice First**: Do a test recording to check audio/video quality
2. **Use a Script**: Write bullet points for what you'll cover
3. **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Actually run your code and show results
4. **Be Prepared**: Have your database seeded and ready before recording
5. **Edit if Needed**: Basic editing to remove mistakes is acceptable
6. **Test Your Link**: Make sure your video link works before submitting
